# Privacy Policy — CyberShield AI

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Application Name:** CyberShield AI  
**Developer/Entity:** CyberShield AI Security Team  

---

## 1. Overview
CyberShield AI is designed with a **privacy-first, local-first** architecture. We believe that your private messages, security checks, and personal data should remain under your control on your device.

## 2. Data Collection & Processing
- **Local Analysis:** All scam message analysis, text pattern checking, heuristic URL inspection, and password strength estimations are performed **locally on your device**.
- **No Message Storage:** We do not upload, store, or sell your private SMS messages, emails, or clipboard contents.
- **Optional Online Lookups:** If you explicitly opt-in to online threat intelligence lookups (such as Google Safe Browsing or breach range queries via k-anonymity), only abstracted cryptographic hashes or non-sensitive request identifiers are transmitted over secure HTTPS connections.
- **Analytics & Crash Reports:** No personal identifiers are collected in analytics. Crash logs are anonymized and used solely to ensure app stability.

## 3. Security Measures
- Secure local storage encryption for app preferences and session state.
- No access to contacts, call logs, or background accessibility services unless explicitly granted for specific security features.

## 4. Contact Us
For privacy questions or concerns, please contact: `privacy@cybershield.ai`
